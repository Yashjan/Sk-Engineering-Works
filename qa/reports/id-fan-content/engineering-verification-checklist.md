# ID Fan / Blower engineering verification checklist

Complete this with engineering and project records before publishing numerical specifications, construction claims or project associations.

## Equipment identity and duty

- [ ] Confirm the commercial product name and whether “ID Fan,” “Industrial Blower,” or both apply.
- [ ] Confirm the actual fan type: centrifugal, axial or another configuration.
- [ ] Confirm the intended service: induced draft, forced/supply air, exhaust, ventilation or another duty.
- [ ] Confirm whether the illustrated and photographed units represent the currently offered configuration.

## Process and performance inputs

- [ ] Required airflow and the conditions at which it is stated.
- [ ] Required static/total pressure and calculated system resistance.
- [ ] Gas or air composition, density, temperature and moisture.
- [ ] Dust loading, particle characteristics and erosion risk.
- [ ] Corrosive conditions and any condensation risk.
- [ ] Operating hours, control range and expected duty cycle.
- [ ] Fan efficiency basis and test method, if any value will be published.
- [ ] Confirmed fan curve and selected operating point.

## Mechanical configuration

- [ ] Impeller type, diameter, blade profile, number of blades and construction.
- [ ] Shaft design, bearing type, lubrication, sealing and cooling if applicable.
- [ ] Motor power, enclosure and efficiency class.
- [ ] Operating RPM.
- [ ] Direct, coupling or belt-drive arrangement and guarding.
- [ ] Inlet and outlet sizes, orientations, transitions and flanges.
- [ ] Damper, flexible connector, expansion joint or inlet-box scope.
- [ ] Installation orientation and direction of rotation.
- [ ] Base frame, anchoring and structural-support requirements.

## Materials, vibration and maintenance

- [ ] Casing, impeller, shaft, base and fastener materials.
- [ ] Surface protection, lining and corrosion/erosion allowances.
- [ ] Balancing standard and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Vibration limits, isolation and monitoring requirements.
- [ ] Noise limits and any attenuation scope.
- [ ] Inspection openings, cleaning access and maintenance clearances.
- [ ] Bearing and drive maintenance instructions.
- [ ] Safe isolation, guarding and lockout provisions included in supply.

## System integration and supply

- [ ] Connected dryer, cyclone, bag filter, duct or exhaust arrangement.
- [ ] Duct loads and allowable nozzle forces.
- [ ] Controls, instrumentation, starter/VFD and interlock scope.
- [ ] Foundation, support, ducting, electrical and installation responsibilities.
- [ ] Applicable design, fabrication, balancing, testing and safety standards.
- [ ] Factory inspection or performance testing included in supply.

## Project and media provenance

- [ ] Confirm what the workshop photograph depicts and when it was recorded.
- [ ] Confirm what each ID Fan / Blower project video depicts.
- [ ] Confirm whether either video is a fabrication record, trial run or completed-unit record.
- [ ] Confirm permission to publish the media and its description.
- [ ] Identify any verified installation without inferring customer or performance details.
- [ ] Confirm whether customer, site or project names may be published.

Until confirmed, do not publish numerical airflow, pressure, speed, motor, temperature, efficiency, material, performance, warranty or project claims.
