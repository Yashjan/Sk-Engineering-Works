# Engineering information needed

This checklist identifies what must be confirmed before deeper public content is written. It is not a request to publish every value; sensitive or variable values can remain enquiry inputs.

## Material handling

### Bucket Elevator
- Belt or chain bucket system; bucket type, spacing and attachment method
- Boot/head/take-up arrangement, drive, backstop and speed/alignment monitoring
- Feed/discharge configuration, access/cleaning and dust containment
- Material condition, bulk density, lump size, lift height and required flow
- Real machine photos, GA drawing and supplied-unit provenance

### Portable Belt Conveyor
- Portable frame, wheels/axles, steering/towing/manual movement and stabilization
- Fixed or adjustable incline/height; safe operating envelope
- Belt, idler, drive/tension arrangement and guards
- Material, flow, loading/discharge points and power source
- Confirmation that the project videos show the offered model

### Hopper
- Receiving duty and usable volume selection inputs
- Wall geometry, discharge gate/feeder, liner and anti-bridging provisions
- Support/load basis, access, guards and dust control
- Real photographs or fabrication drawing

## Milling and size reduction

### Pin Mill
- Actual rotor/stator or pin-disc arrangement and drive
- Feed/product criteria used for selection; heat/dust considerations
- Wear parts, clearances, access and collection system
- Construction/material-contact options and real unit evidence

### Hammer Mill / Crusher
- Confirm whether this is a hammer mill, swing-hammer crusher or another design
- Rotor/hammer/screen arrangement, drive, feed control and discharge
- Wear protection, tramp/overload safeguards and maintenance access
- Intended materials and real model evidence

### Roller Crusher
- Roll count, surface/profile, gap adjustment and drive arrangement
- Feed/product selection data, overload/tramp protection and wear parts
- Guards, access and downstream interface
- Real unit photos/drawings

### Lump Crusher
- Actual crushing mechanism and number/orientation of shafts
- Feed opening, discharge control, drive, guards and overload protection
- Material/lump characteristics used for selection
- Real unit photos/drawings

## Air and dust handling

### ID Fan / Blower
- Fan type, impeller, casing, drive and rotation/flow arrangement
- Airflow/static-pressure selection inputs, temperature and dust loading
- Materials/coatings, balancing/testing and vibration provisions
- Duct/damper/support interfaces and exact media provenance

### Bag Filter
- Filter type, element/media and cleaning mechanism
- Airflow/dust/temperature selection inputs and permissible pressure drop
- Hopper/discharge, fan/duct relationship, controls and fire/static precautions where applicable
- Confirmation of what the fabrication video shows; completed-unit evidence

### Cyclone
- Actual inlet/body/cone/outlet geometry and number of cyclones
- Airflow, particulate and pressure-drop selection basis
- Dust/product discharge arrangement and downstream fan/filter connection
- Materials/lining and confirmation of all photo/video attribution

### Chimney
- Purpose in the supplied system and connected equipment
- Stack geometry/height/diameter design basis and applicable project standards
- Gas temperature/composition/flow inputs; supports, access and foundation interface
- Materials/coatings, fabrication records and media provenance

## Screening, storage and wet process

### Vibro Screen
- Screen type, number of decks, screen media/mesh and motion/drive
- Feed/product cuts and capacity selection inputs
- Isolation mounts, outlets, dust covers, cleaning and maintenance access
- Real unit photos/drawings

### Storage Silos
- Stored material, required holding duty and geometry
- Filling/venting/level indication and discharge/flow-aid equipment
- Support/load/foundation basis and access/safety provisions
- Real unit photos/drawings

### Process Tanks / Washing Tanks
- Separate the offered tank types: holding, settling, washing, brine or mixing
- Vessel geometry, internals/agitation, residence/holding inputs and overflow/outlet arrangement
- Materials/lining, supports, access and connection schedule
- Clarify whether Royal Salt “Tanks” records support this exact route

### Thickener
- Confirm conventional/rake/other thickener design
- Feed well, rake/drive, overflow, underflow and control arrangement
- Feed-solids and settling-test inputs used for selection
- Materials, access and real supplied-unit evidence

### Dryer
- Dryer technology and internal construction
- Heat source, direct/indirect mode, air/material flow and residence control
- Feed/outlet moisture and temperature inputs used in design
- Exhaust/dust system, controls, safeguards and construction options
- Real machine photos, GA/process diagram and project provenance

## Evidence standard for all machines

Before using “fabricated,” “installed,” “completed” or client/project language, retain a company-confirmed record that connects the asset to the machine. External appearance can support visible component descriptions; it cannot prove internal mechanisms, materials, capacity, motor power, efficiency or performance. Native Hindi and Arabic technical review remains required after English engineering approval.
