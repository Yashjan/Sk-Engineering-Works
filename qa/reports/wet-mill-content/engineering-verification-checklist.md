# Wet Mill: engineering verification checklist

Do not publish values or construction claims until S.K. Engineering Works confirms them for the offered Wet Mill design.

| Item to confirm | Required engineering record |
|---|---|
| Actual milling / size-reduction mechanism and working elements | Approved GA, section drawing or engineering note |
| Process-liquid or brine use, introduction, containment and drainage | Process-flow and piping arrangement |
| Capacity in TPH and applicable raw-salt condition | Duty sheet |
| Feed size and target output condition | Process requirement and test basis |
| Motor power, gearbox, coupling and operating speed | Drive schedule |
| Shaft, bearing, seal and lubrication arrangement | Mechanical drawing |
| Material of construction, wear parts and corrosion protection | Approved specification |
| Feed and discharge arrangement, dimensions and support loads | GA / layout drawing |
| Cleaning, inspection and maintenance access | GA and maintenance plan |
| Foundation, electrical, installation, controls and supply scope | Project scope and site interface record |

Also confirm whether the catalog render represents the supplied design and whether verified workshop, installation, CAD or operating records may be published. Do not add capacity, output size, purity, recovery, water consumption, power savings, efficiency, warranty, lead-time, named project or customer claims without that evidence.
