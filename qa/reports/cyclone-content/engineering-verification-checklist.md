# Cyclone engineering verification checklist

Complete this with company engineering and project records before publishing numerical specifications, construction claims or project associations.

## Equipment identity and application

- [ ] Confirm the commercial product name and actual cyclone type.
- [ ] Confirm whether the offered arrangement is a conventional single cyclone, multicyclone or another configuration.
- [ ] Confirm intended applications: dust extraction, process-gas separation, pre-separation, material recovery or other duties.
- [ ] Confirm whether the catalog illustration and site photograph represent currently offered equipment.

## Process and separation basis

- [ ] Required airflow and the conditions at which it is stated.
- [ ] Particle-size distribution, density, shape and abrasiveness.
- [ ] Inlet dust or particle loading and expected variation.
- [ ] Gas composition, density, moisture and condensation risk.
- [ ] Operating and design temperatures.
- [ ] Allowable and calculated pressure loss.
- [ ] Target separation performance, test basis and permitted guarantees.
- [ ] Any confirmed particle cut-size basis.

## Geometry and mechanical construction

- [ ] Inlet dimensions, shape, orientation and connection details.
- [ ] Gas-outlet dimensions and internal outlet or vortex-finder arrangement.
- [ ] Cyclone barrel diameter and height.
- [ ] Cone dimensions, angle and lower outlet size.
- [ ] Number and arrangement of cyclone stages.
- [ ] Casing, cone, duct, flange, support and fastener materials.
- [ ] Wall thicknesses and reinforcement requirements.
- [ ] Wear liners, replaceable wear parts or surface protection, if included.
- [ ] Corrosion allowances and coating or finish.

## Discharge, support and integration

- [ ] Particle collection and discharge method.
- [ ] Rotary airlock, valve, bin, screw or other discharge-device scope.
- [ ] Required gas seal or isolation at the discharge.
- [ ] Support frame, platform, anchoring and structural-load basis.
- [ ] Ducting, flexible connection, damper and expansion-joint scope.
- [ ] Connected fan, dryer, bag filter or other process equipment.
- [ ] Inlet/outlet duct loads and allowable nozzle forces.

## Installation, maintenance and safety

- [ ] Inspection, cleaning and access provisions included in supply.
- [ ] Joint, flange and seal maintenance requirements.
- [ ] Wear-inspection locations and replacement criteria.
- [ ] Safe isolation, dust handling and lockout provisions.
- [ ] Applicable design, fabrication, testing and safety standards.
- [ ] Factory inspection or performance-testing scope.
- [ ] Installation, erection, commissioning and operating responsibilities.

## Project and media provenance

- [ ] Confirm what `plant-structure.webp` depicts, where and when it was recorded, and whether it may be identified as a Cyclone installation.
- [ ] Confirm what the Cyclone fabrication and completed-machine videos depict.
- [ ] Confirm whether `cyclone-field.webp` depicts Cyclone fabrication or other equipment.
- [ ] Confirm publication permission for each asset and caption.
- [ ] Identify verified installations without inferring customer, capacity or performance details.
- [ ] Confirm whether any customer, site or project name may be published.

Until confirmed, do not publish collection efficiency, particle cut size, pressure loss, airflow, temperature limits, material grades, wall thickness, dimensions, performance guarantees, customer installations, certifications, warranty or lead-time claims.
