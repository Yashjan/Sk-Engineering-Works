# Portable Belt Conveyor — engineering verification checklist

Confirm the following before publishing numerical specifications or stronger equipment claims. Unknown values should remain enquiry inputs rather than public claims.

## Equipment identity and mobility

- Actual portable-conveyor model or models offered
- Intended applications and material boundaries
- Wheel count, wheel and axle construction
- Steering arrangement, if any
- Braking or wheel-locking arrangement, if any
- Towing or manual-movement arrangement, if any
- Whether powered travel is offered
- Stabilization and operating-support procedure
- Confirmed relocation method, distance and floor conditions
- Whether incline or discharge height is adjustable
- Adjustment mechanism and safe operating range, if applicable

## Conveyor and drive

- Frame construction and confirmed materials
- Conveyor length and belt width options
- Required capacity and selection basis
- Permissible inclination
- Feed and discharge heights
- Drive arrangement and drive location
- Motor power, electrical supply and controls
- Belt speed and belt material
- Belt support arrangement: idlers, slider bed or other design
- Pulley and bearing construction
- Take-up, tracking and tensioning arrangement
- Feed, side-containment and discharge details

## Service and installation

- Compatible materials, particle sizes, moisture and temperature
- Corrosion, abrasion and outdoor exposure provisions
- Dust-control arrangement
- Guarding, emergency-stop and isolation provisions
- Support, stability and anchoring requirements during operation
- Inspection, cleaning and maintenance access
- Applicable standards and testing records
- Scope of supply and exclusions
- Warranty and delivery terms, if the company chooses to publish them

## Evidence and provenance

- Confirm that both project videos depict the offered Portable Belt Conveyor record
- Confirm whether the catalog image is a render, edited photograph or supplied-unit image
- Confirm whether the equipment-view composite may be presented publicly as illustrative media
- Identify verified project installations, if any, with permission for public attribution
- Retain drawings, equipment records and approvals that support future specifications
