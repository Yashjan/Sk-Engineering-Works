# Bag Filter engineering verification checklist

Confirm from approved drawings, quotations, data sheets, inspection records and responsible engineering personnel before publishing company-specific values or capabilities.

## Equipment identity

- Exact bag-filter or baghouse subtype and intended applications
- Internal gas-flow direction and chamber arrangement
- Cleaning method: pulse jet, reverse air, shaker, manual or another system
- Online or offline cleaning arrangement and operating sequence

## Filtration and duty

- Filter medium, treatment and compatibility limits
- Filter-element dimensions, count, support or cage arrangement
- Total filtration area and design air-to-cloth basis
- Design airflow, inlet dust loading and particle characteristics
- Normal and maximum differential pressure
- Operating temperature, humidity, condensation and chemical limits
- Verified outlet requirement, test method and applicable regulatory basis

## Construction and discharge

- Inlet, outlet and internal baffle arrangement
- Hopper geometry, storage basis and dust-discharge mechanism
- Rotary airlock, screw conveyor, bin or other discharge scope, if any
- Compressed-air receiver, valves and requirements, if applicable
- Construction materials, wall thickness, wear protection and corrosion protection
- Structural design, platforms, ladders, anchoring and maintenance clearances

## System integration and controls

- Fan duty and fan location relative to the filter
- Cyclone, dryer and duct integration for each application
- Differential-pressure instruments and cleaning controls, if supplied
- Electrical scope, panel, PLC or interlocks, if supplied
- Safe shutdown, isolation and filter-media replacement procedure
- Dust hazard assessment, fire/explosion precautions and applicable safety requirements

## Evidence and commercial scope

- Fabrication standards, welding and coating requirements
- Testing, inspection and commissioning records
- Exact scope of supply and exclusions
- Verified completed installations and customer permission
- Provenance and approved public wording for every image and video
- Native technical review of Hindi and Arabic terminology

Do not publish efficiency percentages, micron ratings, emissions guarantees, filter life, pulse-jet cleaning, airflow, pressure, temperature, material, certification, customer or installation claims until the relevant item is confirmed.
